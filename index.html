01 / ABOUT

About

Hi, I'm Muhammed Saahil, a final-year Game Design and Development student with hands-on experience in Unity and Unreal Engine, passionate about building interactive experiences that bring gameplay ideas to life. I enjoy working across the boundary between programming and art: building gameplay systems, custom shaders, and the internal tools that let a team iterate faster. My interests lie at the intersection of game development, gameplay programming, and technical art, where I can combine logical problem-solving with visual creativity to create engaging real-time experiences.

My experience spans gameplay mechanics, player interactions, UI implementation, prototyping, and performance-conscious development. I enjoy taking a concept from an initial idea to a functional, playable experience, experimenting with different approaches, solving technical challenges, and refining the overall player experience through iteration and testing.

Alongside programming, I have experience in 3D modeling, texturing, and real-time asset integration. I'm interested in understanding how assets, materials, shaders, lighting, and engine systems work together to shape a game's visual identity and performance. This combination of development and art allows me to approach projects from both a technical and visual perspective.

Throughout my projects and prototypes, I've explored different gameplay concepts, interactive systems, and development workflows. I'm particularly interested in how thoughtful mechanics, responsive controls, optimized assets, and well-designed interfaces can work together to make a game feel polished and enjoyable. I also enjoy learning new tools and exploring different ways to improve my development process.

I'm looking to grow as a Game Developer, Gameplay Programmer, or Technical Artist, contributing to collaborative projects where I can apply my existing skills, learn from experienced developers, and continue improving my technical and creative abilities. My goal is to build meaningful game experiences while developing a deeper understanding of real-world game production.

MaskOut

I did all of the development and technical art, integrated the UI, animated the character from the provided sprites and integrated the VFX.             Unity
            2026
            Level Design · Technical Art
          

          
Reversal Paradox

          
A 2D puzzle game inspired by Honkai: Star Rail's Golden Scapegoat minigame. After a set number of moves time reverses and your past self repeats everything you did: use it to reach the astrolabe, but never meet it or it's a paradox. I did the level design and all of the development, technical art, UI integration, character animation and VFX/SFX integration (art assets are external). Finished, with 5 to 6 levels.
