Mask the Platforms · Build Your Path
Unity Global Game Jam 2026

MaskOut

A 2D puzzle-platformer built for Global Game Jam around the theme "Mask": hold F and click or drag to mask platforms, reveal hidden routes and make your own way to the exit. I did all of the development and technical art, integrated the UI, animated the character from the provided sprites and integrated the VFX. Finished and playable in your browser on itch.io, with only refinement left.

Your Past Self Repeats Your Moves
Unity 2026 Level Design · Technical Art

Reversal Paradox

A 2D puzzle game inspired by Honkai: Star Rail's Golden Scapegoat minigame. After a set number of moves time reverses and your past self repeats everything you did: use it to reach the astrolabe, but never meet it or it's a paradox. I did the game and level design and all of the development, technical art, UI integration, character animation and VFX/SFX integration (art assets are external). Finished, with 5 to 6 levels.

Play on itch.io ⬇ Download (Windows)
Local Multiplayer · Gameplay Systems
Unreal Engine 5 2026 In Development

GRID-LOCKED: The 99-Tile Blitz

A 2.5D isometric couch co-op brawler for 2 to 4 players on one screen. Plug in gamepads and the game detects them, assigns each player a neon colour and drops everyone into a three-phase match: a territory turf war, a sudden-death Overclock, and an asymmetric Crown Royale. Game design, gameplay programming and animation all done by me.

Look Away · Platforms Rearrange
Unity 2026 Development · Skybox · Technical Art Work in Progress

LOST

A first-person vertical climbing platformer where the world keeps changing: look away from a platform and it can move, rotate or rearrange, so you have to observe, plan and adapt as you climb. I handled the development, the skybox and the technical art. Playable in your browser on itch.io. It is still a work in progress: not finished and not yet refined.
