GRID-LOCKED: The 99-Tile Blitz

A 2.5D isometric couch co-op brawler for 2 to 4 players on one screen. Plug in gamepads and the game detects them, assigns each player a neon colour and drops everyone into a three-phase match: a territory turf war, a sudden-death Overclock, and an asymmetric Crown Royale. Game design, gameplay programming and animation all done by me.

LOST

A first-person vertical climbing platformer where the world keeps changing: look away from a platform and it can move, rotate or rearrange, so you have to observe, plan and adapt as you climb. I handled the development, the skybox and the technical art. Playable in your browser on itch.io. It is still a work in progress: not finished and not yet refined.
